TECHNICAL SEO

Technical SEO Checklist for Modern Websites

Technical SEO creates the foundation that lets search engines discover, process and understand your important pages.

Crawling and indexing

Check robots.txt, XML sitemaps, canonical URLs, status codes and indexability of important pages.

Site architecture

Keep important content easy to reach and use descriptive internal links to establish clear relationships between pages.

Performance and mobile

Reduce unnecessary scripts, optimize assets and ensure the experience works well across screen sizes.

Structured data

Use valid structured data where it genuinely describes the page, such as Article, BreadcrumbList or Organization/Person information.
