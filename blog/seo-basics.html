What Is SEO? A Practical Beginner Guide

SEO helps people discover useful pages through search engines. The strongest foundation combines helpful content, accessible technical structure and an understanding of what people are trying to find.

1. Start with search intent

Before writing, identify the problem behind a search. A beginner question, a product comparison and a request to buy something need different types of pages.

2. Make important pages discoverable

Use clear navigation, descriptive internal links, sensible URLs and an XML sitemap. Check that important pages can be crawled and indexed.

3. Publish genuinely useful content

Answer the reader’s question directly, organize information with descriptive headings and include details that help people make progress.

4. Measure and improve

Use Google Search Console to review search impressions, clicks and queries. Use analytics to understand what visitors do after landing on your site.
