AI SEO: Preparing Content for AI Search

AI search is evolving, but the fundamentals remain familiar: publish reliable information, explain ideas clearly and make your website accessible to search systems and people.

1. Answer questions clearly

Use descriptive headings and concise opening answers, then add context, examples and evidence. Avoid vague filler that makes the useful information hard to find.

2. Build connected topic coverage

Create related pages that answer different questions within a topic. Link them together naturally so readers can continue learning.

3. Show who is responsible for the content

Use clear authorship, keep facts current and cite credible sources where appropriate. Never claim expertise, results or endorsements that cannot be verified.

4. Keep technical foundations healthy

Check crawling, indexability, mobile usability, page performance and valid structured data. Structured data should match what visitors can actually see.
