Keyword Research: From Seed to Search Intent

Keyword research is not just collecting search phrases. It is the process of understanding what people need and mapping those needs to useful pages on your website.

1. Create seed topics

List your main services, subjects, audience questions and problems. Expand these into related phrases using search suggestions and keyword tools.

2. Group phrases by intent

Separate informational, commercial, navigational and transactional searches. Similar intent usually belongs on the same page; different intent may need a different page.

3. Prioritize opportunities

Consider relevance, competition, likely value, the current search results and whether you can create a page that is genuinely better for the reader.

4. Map keywords to pages

Give each important page a clear primary purpose. Build supporting content where it adds value and connect related pages with descriptive internal links.
