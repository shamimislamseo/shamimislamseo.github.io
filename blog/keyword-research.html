KEYWORD RESEARCH

Keyword Research: From Seed to Search Intent

Effective keyword research connects what people search for with the pages you can build to satisfy their needs.

Start with seed topics

List the core products, services, problems and questions related to your website.

Group by intent

Separate informational, commercial, navigational and transactional opportunities and map them to suitable page types.

Prioritize

Consider relevance, competition, business value, existing authority and the quality of the current search results.

Build topic clusters

Create a strong primary page and supporting content, then connect them with descriptive internal links.
